A guide to everything the PungusSouls Reborn mod adds to Valheim: weapons and their real numbers, armor sets, titanite and embers, the blacksmith's upgrade ladder, new creatures and bosses, companions, locations and build pieces.

Stats are read from the mod's item prefabs and recipes from its registration code. Damage is base damage at +0, with the gain per upgrade level in brackets. Server configuration can change any of these numbers.

## Getting started

Your journey starts like any other in the tenth realm, but a small site sits just ahead of the starting circle. Do not install the mod on an existing world; saves made with it are not safe to load after uninstalling.

1. **Read the strange runes** beside the spawn. Touching the runestone marks Firelink Shrine, the hub, on your map.
2. **Beat Eikthyr**, then travel to Firelink Shrine in the Meadows. You can rest and sleep there but cannot build.
3. **Talk to Andre** at the Blacksmith Altar to craft and upgrade weapons and armor.
4. **Check Shalquoir's map** to mark where companions can be found. Once unlocked they stay with you for the rest of the game.
5. **Collect titanite** from enemies and mineable remains. Each biome has its own grade, from Titanite Shard in the Black Forest to Titanite Scale in the Deep North.
6. **Bring Andre embers**, dropped by bosses, to raise the altar's crafting level and unlock the next tier.

### Titanite remains

Giant weapons, helmets, skulls and ribs half-buried in the world can be mined for titanite: ancient swords and helmets in the Black Forest and Swamps, felled giants' skulls in the Mountains, a fallen god's ribs in the Plains, and titanite veins in the Ashlands and Deep North. Twinkling titanite rocks appear in the Mistlands.

### Companion quick guide

Interact with a new companion to wake and register them. **E** opens their inventory and **Shift + E** opens their command menu. The **Companion Control Horn** lets you pick a companion and command or summon them from anywhere.

Orders include Follow, Idle, Stay Home and Set Home, with Passive, Defensive or Aggressive tactics. Supported companions can be mounted (**Alt + E**) or hitched to carts (**Ctrl + E**).

A fallen companion leaves a tombstone with a Resurrect prompt. The Firekeeper at Firelink Shrine upgrades their health, stamina and carry weight.

| Job | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Hunt / Patrol | Hunts or patrols the home area |
| Gathering | Collects nearby pickables |
| Lumbering | Cuts wood |
| Mining / Quarrying | Works ore and stone |
| Fishing | Fishes from a suitable shore |
| Repair | Repairs damaged structures |
| Farming | Plants and harvests crops |
| Smelting | Feeds stations and empties their output |
| Crafting | Cooks and crafts from work orders, by amount or to keep a stock |
